# Contributing to Awesome JEV

Anything about Jev or System One models is welcome: papers, open models, projects built with Jev, benchmarks and articles. We only turn down ads and links that have nothing to do with Jev.

## How to add an entry

Add one line to the section of `README.md` that fits best, then open a pull request. We tidy the wording, the badges and the counts at the top of the README after merging, so you can leave the numbers alone.

~~~markdown
- [Name](link), One short clause on what it is. [badges]
~~~

For a paper, put its title in quotes after the name.

~~~markdown
- [RouteLLM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.18665), "Learning to Route LLMs with Preference Data". [badges]
~~~

Badges are optional. Copy them from a nearby entry if you want them.
